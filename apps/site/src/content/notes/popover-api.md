title: "the popover attribute"
date: 2025-06-16
tags: [web]
---

`<button popovertarget="menu">Menu</button> <div id="menu" popover>...</div>` toggles without JS, closes on outside click and Escape, sits on the top layer above everything.

Baseline since spring 2024. Tooltips and menus got a lot simpler.
